How it works

  • Enter how much you want to send in Canadian dollars.
  • Pick the currency your recipient will receive.
  • The app fetches the live mid-market rate and applies each provider's fee + rate margin to show what actually arrives.
  • Providers are ranked — the top one puts the most money in your recipient's hands.

Your data

Everything is stored on your phone only. No account, no server, nothing leaves your device except the public exchange-rate lookup.

Disclaimer

Comparisons are estimates based on the fee and rate-margin figures you enter. Transfer services change their pricing often — always verify the final receive amount in the provider's own app before sending money.
